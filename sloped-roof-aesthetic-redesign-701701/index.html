Who We Are

Roofing design that respects the material

Cedar is one of the most durable and naturally beautiful roofing materials available. Our team specialises in working with existing sloped cedar roofs: assessing their structure, improving their visual impact, and expanding the usable floor area beneath them.

Whether you want a cleaner roofline, better proportions, or a significantly enlarged enclosed floor plate, we work from the existing frame outward so you keep what works and gain what you need.

Photo: cedar shingle detail, natural grain texture

What We Do

Redesign services

Aesthetic Improvement

We refine rooflines, adjust pitch angles, and propose material upgrades that lift the overall look of the structure without requiring a full replacement.

Floor Plate Expansion

By restructuring roof geometry and extending eaves or dormers, we create larger, fully enclosed floor areas that add real usable square footage.

Cedar Maintenance Planning

Cedar needs the right treatment schedule to last. We assess the current condition of your cedar and build a maintenance and refinishing plan into the redesign from the start.

3D Design and Visualisation

Before any work starts, you see a full 3D rendering of the proposed redesign. Review proportions, materials, and finishes from every angle before committing.

How It Works

From existing roof to finished redesign

  1. 01

    Site Assessment

    We measure the existing roof structure, assess the condition of the cedar, and document the current floor plate. This gives us an accurate baseline to design from.

  2. 02

    Design Development

    Our designers propose roof geometry changes that improve the aesthetic and create the additional enclosed floor area you need. We present multiple options with clear trade-offs.

  3. 03

    3D Visualisation and Review

    You receive detailed 3D renderings of the preferred design. We revise until the proportions, material finishes, and roofline look exactly right to you.

  4. 04

    Construction Documentation

    We produce full technical drawings, AutoCAD files, and specifications your builder needs to execute the redesign accurately and to code.
