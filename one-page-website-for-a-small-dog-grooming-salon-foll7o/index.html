About Us

We treat every dog like our own

Paws & Whiskers is a small, independent grooming salon right here in Brighton. We started because we believe every dog deserves to feel comfortable, calm and cared for during their groom, not just at the end of it.

We keep our appointment slots small so you are never rushed, and your dog always gets our full attention. Whether it is their very first groom or they are a regular, we take the time to make sure they feel safe and happy throughout.
