About DigitalVault

Quality digital resources, delivered the moment you need them

What We Do

DigitalVault is a curated marketplace of premium digital products built for creators, freelancers and small teams. Every product in our catalogue is hand-reviewed before it goes on sale, because we would rather have fifty excellent items than five hundred average ones.

When you purchase from us, your download link arrives in your inbox the moment your payment clears, protected by a secure, time-limited token. There are no accounts to create, no hoops to jump through.

Our Standards

  • Every file is tested and scanned before listing
  • Licences are clearly stated on every product page
  • Download links are never publicly accessible URLs
  • Payments processed by Stripe and PayPal, never stored by us
  • GDPR-compliant data handling across the EU and UK

Quick Answers

How do I receive my download?

As soon as your payment is confirmed, an email is sent to the address you provided at checkout. It contains a secure link that is valid for 48 hours and up to 5 download attempts. If you need it re-sent, contact support.

Can I use a product in client work?

Most products include a Standard Licence covering personal and commercial use for one end product. Extended licences for unlimited projects are available on selected items. Always check the licence tab on the product page.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (via Stripe), as well as PayPal and Apple Pay / Google Pay where supported. All transactions are encrypted with TLS and 3D Secure where applicable.

What is your refund policy?

Because digital files are delivered immediately, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis. If a file is corrupted, mislabelled or not as described, we will resolve it promptly. See the full Refund Policy.
