Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about buying and downloading from DigitalVault.

Downloads & Delivery

How do I receive my download after purchase?

Within seconds of a successful payment, an automated email is sent to the address you entered at checkout. The email contains a secure link that is valid for 48 hours and up to 5 download attempts. If you need it reissued, contact support with your order number.

The link in my email says it has expired. What do I do?

Contact us through the support form with your order number and the email address used at checkout. We will issue a new link within one business day.

I didn't receive a confirmation email. What should I check?

First, check your spam or junk folder and search for "DigitalVault". If it isn't there, double-check the email address you entered at checkout. Then contact support and we will locate your order and re-send.

What file formats will I receive?

Each product page lists the included formats in the description. Common formats include PDF, DOCX, FIGMA, SVG, PNG and ZIP archives. Check the product listing before purchase.

Payment & Security

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All transactions are encrypted with TLS 1.3 and 3D Secure where supported by your bank.

Is my card information stored on your servers?

No. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. DigitalVault never sees or stores your full card number.

Do prices include VAT or sales tax?

Displayed prices are exclusive of tax. Applicable VAT or sales tax is calculated and shown at checkout before you confirm payment. The rate depends on your billing country.

Licensing

What does the Standard Licence allow?

The Standard Licence permits personal and commercial use for one end product (a website, a document you send to a client, a product you sell once). You may not redistribute or resell the digital file itself.

Can I use a product across multiple client projects?

A Standard Licence covers one end product per purchase. If you need to use the same product across multiple client deliverables, you need an Extended Licence, which is available on selected products. Contact us if you need clarification for a specific product.

Refunds & Issues

Can I get a refund on a digital product?

Because files are delivered immediately, we do not offer refunds as a matter of course. However, if a file is corrupted, missing content, or significantly different from the description, we will either fix the issue or issue a full refund. See the Refund Policy for full details.

A file I downloaded won't open. What should I do?

Check you have the required software for the file format listed on the product page. If the file appears to be corrupted or is genuinely unreadable, contact support and we will replace it.
