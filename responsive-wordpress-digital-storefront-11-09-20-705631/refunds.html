Digital products and the right to cancel

Under EU and UK consumer law, the right to cancel a digital download purchase is waived once you have started the download or explicitly acknowledged that delivery begins immediately upon purchase. By completing checkout you confirm this acknowledgement.

We do not therefore offer refunds for downloaded files simply because you changed your mind or bought the wrong product. Please read product descriptions carefully before purchasing.

When we will issue a refund

We will issue a full refund or replacement in the following circumstances:

  • The file you received is corrupted and cannot be opened.
  • The file content is materially and significantly different from the product description on the listing.
  • The download link failed to work and we were unable to provide a working replacement within 48 hours of your support request.
  • You were charged twice for the same order due to a technical error on our side.

How to request a refund

Contact us via the support form within 14 days of purchase. Include your order number, the email address used at checkout, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within one business day and to resolve any valid claim within three business days.

Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback without contacting us first, your access to future purchases may be restricted pending resolution. We always prefer to resolve issues directly and quickly.
